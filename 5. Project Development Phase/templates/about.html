🌱 About OptiCrop

OptiCrop is an AI-powered Smart Agricultural Production Optimization Engine that helps farmers identify the most suitable crop based on soil nutrients and environmental conditions using Machine Learning.

🎯 Project Objective

The objective of OptiCrop is to support farmers by recommending the most suitable crop based on Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), temperature, humidity, pH, and rainfall. The system aims to improve agricultural productivity, optimize resource utilization, and promote sustainable farming practices.

✨ Key Features

  • AI-based Crop Recommendation
  • Analysis of Soil Nutrients (N, P, K)
  • Weather and Environmental Analysis
  • Crop Information with Growing Tips
  • Simple, Fast and User-Friendly Interface
