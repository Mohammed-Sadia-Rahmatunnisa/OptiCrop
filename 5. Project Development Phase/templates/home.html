🌱 About OptiCrop

OptiCrop is a Machine Learning-based crop recommendation system that analyzes soil nutrients and environmental conditions to recommend the most suitable crop for cultivation.

The system considers Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), Temperature, Humidity, pH, and Rainfall to provide intelligent crop recommendations.

Our goal is to improve agricultural productivity, reduce crop failure, and encourage sustainable farming practices through Artificial Intelligence.
